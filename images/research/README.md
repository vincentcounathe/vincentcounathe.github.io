# Research figures

Figures are copied or extracted from the existing local paper assets. No experimental data, plotted curves, graph structure, or labels were changed.

## QUASAR

- `quasar-gradients.png` and `quasar-gradients.svg`: unchanged copies of `quasar/assets/training-mismatch.png` and `.svg`.
- Original paper Figure 1, “Training through the reconstruction,” from `figures/borrowed_gradient_pair_vF.pdf` in arXiv:2608.13966v1.
- The thumbnail is the original 2400 × 1175 PNG. Selecting it opens the full vector SVG, preserving crisp labels when enlarged.
- The two loss surfaces compare the gradient computed at reconstructed weights and applied to latent weights in Standard QAT and QUASAR.
- Original source and the source export’s canvas repair are documented in `quasar/assets/README.md`. Neither asset was cropped, recolored, or redrawn for the homepage.

## Leave-One-Out Transformers

- `loo-transformers.svg`: unchanged original vector from `neurips2026_preprint/figures/dependency_patterns.svg` in the paper's Overleaf source, commit `1fcb2332c691dec79e89467fcbdc05b9576c8cd7`.
- Figure 1 on page 2 of *Empowering Masked Diffusion Models to Self-Correct with Leave-One-Out Transformers*.
- Compares input-output dependencies and training signal in causal, bidirectional, and leave-one-out models.
- `loo-transformers.png`: 2475 × 940 PNG rendered from the SVG with `rsvg-convert -w 2475 -h 940`. No cropping, recoloring, or redrawing.
- Selecting the thumbnail opens the original SVG for crisp enlargement.
